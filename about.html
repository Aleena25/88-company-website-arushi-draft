Our work

Khan Baba Restaurant

Khan Baba Restaurant wanted custom glass bottles that reflected the atmosphere of their restaurant while also promoting their takeaway and catering services. We created branded glass water bottles featuring their logo, contact details, and restaurant branding directly on the bottle.

The design was made to stand out on restaurant tables without feeling overcrowded. The final result gave the restaurant a more professional dining in and take away experience while also acting as subtle advertising for customers dining in.

The bottles are now ideal for table service, catering events, and restaurant photography, helping the brand feel more premium and memorable.

AtoZ Removal & Cleaners

For AtoZ Removal & Cleaners, the aim was to create fun, approachable branding that still looked professional. We designed customised bottles using their mascot logo to make the brand instantly recognisable and friendly.

Different bottle styles were created for office use, promotional giveaways, and staff branding. The brighter colours and playful design helped the bottles stand out while still keeping a clean and professional look.

This project showed how customised bottles can work beyond restaurants and corporate spaces, helping service businesses improve brand recognition in a simple but effective way.

Adam Bernard Solicitors

Adam Bernard Solicitors required premium customised water bottles that matched the professional and modern image of their legal practice. The objective was to create elegant branded bottles suitable for office meetings, reception areas, corporate events, and client hospitality.

We designed sleek glass bottles with a minimal vertical branding layout featuring the company logo and brand colours. The clean and sophisticated design helped reinforce the firm's premium image while improving presentation within their office environment. The bottles now serve as both a practical office product and a subtle yet effective branding tool for clients and visitors.

How ordering works

All orders and enquiries are handled directly by email, to keep the service simple and personal.

  1. Send your enquiry

    Email us with your quantity and branding requirements.

  2. Share your logo or artwork

    Send your logo or design files, and we'll create a visual mock-up for your approval.

  3. Approve your design

    Once you're happy with the mock-up and bottle selection, we'll confirm your order and provide a production timeline.

  4. Production & delivery

    Your customised bottles are produced, quality-checked, and delivered directly to your location.
